Pool & Parking Rules and Regulations

Please read carefully before using the complex facilities. All guests are required to abide by these rules.

🏊 Pool Rules
⏰ Pool Hours: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM
No Lifeguard on Duty. All persons use the pool and gym at their own risk. Children under 16 are strictly prohibited in pool and gym areas without adult supervision. Ages 16–18 must have written permission from their parents.
All persons must shower before entering the pool using the showers installed near the pool for this purpose.
Smoking and vaping are NOT permitted in any public common area of the complex.
No glass of any kind in the pool or deck area under any circumstance.
Food in small portions stored in sealed plastic containers may be brought to the pool area but can only be consumed at the tables in covered areas. No food at poolside or at the loungers. Drinks permitted in plastic or aluminum containers only.
No diving, jumping, running or pushing in pool areas. Keep noise to a minimum so as not to interfere with the peaceful enjoyment of other guests.
Please remove your towel and personal items whenever you leave the pool area. Chairs may not be reserved for the day.
Pool use is restricted to condo owners (or persons authorized to rent the condo) and registered guests staying in the unit.
The Condominium Association and owners assume no responsibility for lost or stolen items, nor for any accident or injuries related to use of the pool or gym.
🚗 Parking Rules
Unoccupied units are not allowed to occupy a parking stall.
Only one parking stall per unit can be used at a time.
The parking gate must be closed after each use.
Parking stalls can only be used by vehicles in working condition.
It is prohibited to park outside a designated parking stall.
⚠️ Violation Penalties (SOLANA By-Laws)
Monetary Fines Applied to the Homeowner
➤ 1st notice of violation ignored → $2,000 MXN fine
➤ 2nd notice of violation ignored → $5,000 MXN fine
➤ 3rd notice of violation ignored → Legal action against homeowner

Notification of violations will be posted on the condo door and emailed to the homeowner. It is the homeowner's responsibility to address fine violations and associated costs with their renters, guests, and/or property management company.

By staying at Suite Solana (Condo B-201), all guests acknowledge having read and agreed to these rules as part of their registration. Thank you for your cooperation — we look forward to your visit.
